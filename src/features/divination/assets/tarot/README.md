# Rider–Waite–Smith card images

The 78 faces are Pamela Colman Smith’s illustrations from the 1909 Roses & Lilies edition published by Rider & Co. The card back is the matching historical Roses & Lilies pattern. The original deck was scanned by Saskia Jansen.

- [Wikimedia Commons image set](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck_(Roses_%26_Lilies))
- [Example face and public-domain notice](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS1909_-_00_Fool.jpeg)
- [Back scan and public-domain notice](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Waite%E2%80%93Smith_Tarot_Roses_and_Lilies_cropped.jpg)

The JPEGs are at most 330 pixels wide and are bundled with the app. Some scans were obtained as resized copies of the same Commons files from [Arcanebra](https://arcanebra.com/methodology/). Filenames match the card IDs in the deck. The source files number each suit from ace (01) through king (14).
